Virtual dating, a dating simulator, and sharp-tongued Korean grandmas — all in one AI chat app.
Maldongmu ("chat companion" in Korean) lets you sit down with one of 1,000,000 AI personas
built from real Korean demographic data. Not a chatbot that answers questions —
a neighbor you just met who somehow feels like an old friend.

Write in English, Japanese, Chinese or Korean — every character replies in your language.
(App menus are currently in Korean; an English interface is on the way.)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💗 Virtual dating with an affection meter

Pick a gender and an age range, and you're seated at a quiet café for a blind date.
From an awkward hello to getting to know each other, to a spark —
the relationship deepens one step at a time, never rushed.

- An affection meter that moves with every message: warmth and sincerity open
  their heart; rudeness or low effort makes them pull back
- Not a doll that likes you no matter what — a person with their own standards
- Today's date is one of 1M people with their own age, job and regional accent
- Great for practicing dating conversation in Korean or your own language

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔥 Sharp-tongued grandmas from every region of Korea

Seoul, Busan, Jeolla, Chungcheong, Jeju — each region's "cursing grandma."
She scolds you in thick dialect, then makes sure you've eaten.
A very Korean kind of affection, and a fun way to hear real regional speech.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💬 AI conversations that feel like real people

- 1,000,000 AI characters, each with a different age, hometown, job and personality
- Natural dialogue with regional dialects and generational speech intact
- Meet people you rarely get to: judges, firefighters, haenyeo sea divers, pilots
- From a twenty-year-old's worries to a seventy-year-old's wisdom
- A gentle way to practice Korean: switch to Korean any time and they'll follow

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🏡 How to enjoy it

- Virtual dating: pick gender and age range, start a blind date
- Meet today's match: a random neighbor, no spoilers
- Three-way chat: invite two friends and talk together
- Today's neighbors: new people introduced every day
- Got something on your mind? Pick a worry and we'll find the right companion
- Find neighbors: search by region, age and gender
- Neighbor notebook: your collection of everyone you've met
